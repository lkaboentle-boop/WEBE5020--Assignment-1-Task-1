Sweet Creations By Soso Story

We are a small bakery owned by Kagiso Moruti. The business was created from a passion for baking and creating delicious homemade treats.

What started as baking treatsfor family and friends developed into a small business offering quality jam gtarts and different desing of biscuits to customers.

Our Mission

To provide delicious, affordable and high-quality homemade baked goods while ensuring customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To grow Sweet Creations By Soso into a trusted local bakery known for quality products and excellent customer service.
